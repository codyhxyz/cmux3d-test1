# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and agent operators working across several concurrent local shell sessions who want spatial orientation without losing direct terminal control.

## Product Purpose

CMUX3D turns six independent local shells into one rotatable workspace. Success means users can identify, focus, and operate any shell as quickly as they would in a conventional terminal multiplexer.

## Brand Personality

Technical, precise, dimensional. The interface should feel like a serious instrument rather than a themed terminal demo.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic glass dashboards, neon cyberpunk noise, fake terminal output, decorative controls, and motion that slows access to a shell.

## Design Principles

- The terminals are real; visual spectacle never blocks input.
- Spatial position should strengthen session memory.
- Every control must remain legible and conventional.
- Render effects should communicate focus and depth.
- Keep the local-shell trust boundary visible and narrow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and focus visibility. Support keyboard focus, reduced motion, touch input, and instructions that do not rely on color alone.
